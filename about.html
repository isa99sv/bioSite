Patrick Stewart is stage and film actor well-known for multiple popular works of media including central roles in the “Star Trek” and “X-Men” franchises as well as supporting roles across many films, shows, and video games. Stewart was born in Mirfield, England on July 13, 1940, and would begin studying acting and drama at the age of 11. He would later make his professional stage debut in 1959 at the age of 19, from this point Stewart primarily worked as a stage actor with a few minor TV and film roles before landing the role of “Capt. Jean-Luc Picard” in the science-fiction series “Star Trek: The Next Generation” in 1987. The series would later go on to become an international success and make Stewart a household name which would lead him to take on many more film roles, however he would also continue stage acting as well with his most popular performances being roles in “Shakespeare” plays. Throughout his acting career he has won twenty-eight awards including thirteen best performance related awards and over fifty nominations.
